About Gesture AI

Context-Aware Gesture-to-Speech Assistive System

Project Objective

Gesture AI is designed as an assistive communication system that converts hand gestures into meaningful text and speech. The project combines computer vision, machine learning, facial emotion recognition and contextual reasoning.
